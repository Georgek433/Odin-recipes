Pancakes

Description

These delicous pancakes are perfect for a Saturday morning breakfast after a run

They only take around 20 minutes to make and use common household ingredients

Ingredients

  • 1 cup flour
  • 1 egg
  • 1 cup of milk

Steps

  1. Mix the dry ingredients in a bowl
  2. Whisk in the egg and milk
  3. Cook on a hot pan until golden on both sides
